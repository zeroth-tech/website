Why the name Zeroth

Before the first,
a deeper foundation.

Our name comes from Isaac Asimov’s Zeroth Law of Robotics. Three established laws revealed the need for a deeper foundation: a principle that took precedence and expanded what those laws could accomplish.

That idea is central to how we work. Sometimes progress begins by revisiting the foundations.
