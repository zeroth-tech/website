PRIVACY

A clear view of your information.

Last updated November 9, 2026

This notice covers Zeroth Technology, Inc.’s informational website, including the Our Writings and archive pages. The original blog, linked services, and portfolio-company products are described separately below.

Browsing these pages

These pages have no account-registration form, advertising trackers, or application analytics. The background animation runs in your browser. Pointer movement and clicks used by that animation are not sent to us. The website code does not set cookies.

If you use Accessibility, your Stop animation and Easy-read choices are saved in this browser’s local storage so they apply across pages and later visits. These preferences are not sent to us. You can change them in Accessibility or remove them by clearing this site’s browser data. Your device’s reduced-motion setting is also respected.

Delivering the website

Loading a page sends ordinary technical information, such as your IP address, requested URL, and browser information, to the services delivering it. The public site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and delivery providers may process operational logs to serve pages, protect their systems, and diagnose problems. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not use the page animation to collect this information.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the technical information needed for those requests. See the Google Fonts FAQ. Provider handling and retention are governed by their own policies.

The original blog and comments

Our Writings links to articles and archives on the separately hosted original blog. That blog is configured to use Google Analytics for visit measurement and has consent controls for Google Analytics and GitHub features. Its enabled integrations and your choices can affect the information processed. Google Analytics can process browsing events and browser or device information; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Blog comments use Giscus and GitHub Discussions. If you choose to comment or react, your contribution is associated with your GitHub account and may be publicly visible, along with your username and public profile details. This involves more than a GitHub ID: it includes the comment or reaction you submit. GitHub handles account sign-in and any authorization requested by Giscus. Signing in through Giscus stores an encrypted authentication token in your browser’s local storage. You can also participate directly on GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Giscus’s Privacy Policy.

The original blog also loads third-party fonts and script resources. Those providers receive requests needed to deliver their resources. Opening an article, enabling an integration, or following an external link can therefore involve processing separate from these informational pages.

If you contact us

Email links open your email app; they do not send a message automatically. If you write to us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond and handle your request. Email providers also process the message. Please do not send passwords or sensitive records through an ordinary email link.

Portfolio companies and your choices

Our subsidiaries’ products and services have their own data practices and policies. Read the relevant policy before using a service, including Pyrana’s Privacy Policy and the privacy information linked from Vero’s website.

For questions about information you have sent us, or a request to access, correct, or delete it, contact jamey@zeroth.technology. We will consider your request under applicable law and any recordkeeping obligations. You can manage GitHub contributions and authorizations in GitHub and browser-stored settings in your browser. We may update this notice as the site changes; the date above identifies this version.
